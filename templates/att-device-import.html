    Attendance Report
    Upload attendance file

    Export from the attendance device and upload here. Accepted: .dat, .txt, .csv, .xlsx. Each row needs the employee's Device ID and a date-time. Old .xls files must be saved as .xlsx or .csv first.

    How the day is recorded
    • First punch of the day = In time, last punch = Out time in Daily Attendance.
    • 1 punch in a day = Half Day; first punch later than in-time + late minutes = Late Present; last punch 30+ minutes before out-time = Early Leave; otherwise Present.

    In / out time and late minutes come from Attendance Settings for that weekday, otherwise 09:00–18:00 with 15 minutes grace.
